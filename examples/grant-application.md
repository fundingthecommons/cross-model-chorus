# Tallowmere Collective: application to the Fernquill Open Tools Fund

**Ask:** USD 85,000 over 12 months, from March 2027.

## Summary

Tallowmere Collective builds open-source tools that help small community land trusts keep
track of the land, buildings and agreements they hold in common. Since our founding in 2024,
the number of trusts using our tracker has grown from 3 to 4,000, a 1,300-fold increase in two
years. This grant will take the tracker from a working prototype to a tool any trust can run
on its own.

## The problem

Most community land trusts keep their records in spreadsheets maintained by one volunteer.
When that volunteer leaves, the trust can lose track of leases, repair obligations and the
terms of its own agreements. Our survey of trusts found that most had lost at least one
important record in the past five years.

## What we will do

- **Months 1 to 4:** rebuild the tracker's data model and publish an import tool for
  spreadsheets.
- **Months 5 to 8:** run six regional workshops for trust volunteers.
- **Months 9 to 12:** release version 1.0 and hand maintenance to a steering group of trusts.

Our pilot with three trusts finished in June 2026, two months after it began in August 2026,
and showed that volunteers could learn the tool in an afternoon.

## Outcomes

- 1,200 volunteers trained across six workshops.
- Version 1.0 released under an open licence.
- A steering group of five trusts in place to maintain it.
